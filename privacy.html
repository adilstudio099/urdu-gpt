1. Who we are

Urdu GPT is an Android app that provides an Urdu AI assistant with online chat, offline on-device chat and a voice assistant called Jarvis. This policy explains what information the app handles and why.

2. Information we collect

  • Google account details: when you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, profile picture and a unique account ID through Firebase Authentication.
  • Profile details you enter: your full name and gender. They are used to address you correctly in AI replies.
  • Your API keys: the Gemini and OpenRouter keys you paste into the app. They are saved under your account so you do not have to enter them again on a new phone.
  • Online chats and settings: your online chat history (messages and titles) and your app settings are saved under your account.
  • Usage and device information: basic app usage statistics through Firebase Analytics (for example app version, device model, Android version and a random app-instance ID). We do not put chat text, keys or other personal content into analytics events.

3. What stays on your phone

  • Offline chats, and images or audio you attach to the offline model, are processed on your device and are not uploaded by the app.
  • The offline model file, your Vault notes and your History log are stored on your device.

4. AI providers and third parties

  • Online chat (OpenRouter): your messages are sent to OpenRouter using your own key and are routed to a model provider. Each provider has its own privacy practices; some free models may log prompts. Do not enter sensitive information.
  • Jarvis (Google Gemini): your voice or text requests, and screen content when you use features such as skill learning, are sent to the Google Gemini API using your own key, under Google's terms and privacy policy.
  • Firebase (Google): provides sign-in, the database that stores your synced data, and analytics.
  • Hugging Face: when you download the offline model, the file is downloaded from Hugging Face, which can see your IP address.

5. Permissions the app may ask for

  • Internet access, for sign-in, sync, online chat and model download.
  • Microphone, for voice input in Jarvis.
  • Notifications and foreground service, to show model download progress.
  • Accessibility service and screen capture, only if you turn on device-control and skill-learning features in Jarvis.
  • Photo or file access, only for the images or audio you choose to attach.

6. Storage and security

Synced data is stored in Google Firebase (Firestore). Access rules allow only the signed-in owner of an account to read or write that account's data. No system is perfectly secure, and your API keys are stored for your convenience, so use keys with limits where possible and revoke a key if you think it is exposed.

7. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information and we do not share it with advertisers. Data is shared only with the services listed above, as needed for the app to work.

8. Your choices and deleting your data

  • You can sign out in the app at any time. Signing out removes synced data, keys and online chats from that phone.
  • You can change your name and gender in your profile.
  • To delete your account and the data stored under it, email us from the address of your Google account at the contact address below. We will delete it within a reasonable time.

9. Children

Urdu GPT is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.
